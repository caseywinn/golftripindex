## State-Specific Notes

### Florida
- Mention: Florida is the most versatile golf state (year-round play, huge variety)
- GTI trips: Streamsong (inland, minimalist architecture) and Cabot Citrus Farms (newer, premium)
- Competitor angle: FairwayConcierge and GolfDigest both rank Myrtle Beach first; GTI focuses on course quality, not volume
- Backlink target: Visit Florida (visitflorida.com), Florida Sports Foundation

### Michigan
- Mention: Michigan is the most underrated public golf state in the US (650+ public courses)
- GTI trips: Arcadia Bluffs (Lake Michigan, links style), potential Forest Dunes nearby
- Competitor angle: Michigan articles focus on northern Michigan, often skip Arcadia Bluffs pricing details
- Backlink target: Travel Michigan (michigan.org/golf), Arcadia Bluffs media page

### Wisconsin
- Mention: Wisconsin competes with Kohler's global reputation AND Sand Valley's modern minimalism
- GTI trips: Sand Valley (5 courses, minimalist, budget-friendlier), Kohler (luxury, Ryder Cup venue)
- Competitor angle: Most Wisconsin content talks about Whistling Straits and stops there
- Backlink target: Travel Wisconsin (travelwisconsin.com), Sand Valley press

### North Carolina
- Mention: Pinehurst is American golf's spiritual home — 40+ courses in a 20-mile radius
- GTI trips: Pinehurst (9 resort courses + surrounding Sandhills area)
- Competitor angle: USAToday 10Best ranks Pinehurst in top 10 but gives generic overview
- Backlink target: Visit NC (visitnc.com/golf), Pinehurst media/press page

### Oregon
- Mention: Bandon Dunes is the benchmark for American links golf — 5 courses, no distractions, walking only
- GTI trips: Bandon Dunes (highest-ranked GTI destination)
- Competitor angle: All competitors agree Bandon is elite; GTI adds which courses to prioritize and honest booking logistics
- Backlink target: Travel Oregon (traveloregon.com), Bandon Dunes media page
