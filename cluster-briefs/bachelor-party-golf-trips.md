### Top Destinations (main section, ~900 words)

**1. Scottsdale, Arizona — Best Overall** (~200 words)
- Why: the ideal golf + nightlife balance; 200+ courses, world-class desert layouts
- Best courses: TPC Scottsdale (Stadium), Troon North, Whisper Rock (if connected)
- Nightlife: Old Town bars, Tempe, resort pool scenes
- Best time: October-April (avoid summer)
- Cost estimate: $1,200-1,800/person for 3 days
- Group tip: 8-12 people is the sweet spot; book 6+ months out for peak months

**2. Myrtle Beach, South Carolina — Best for Mixed Groups** (~200 words)
- Why: volume play; 90+ courses accommodates any skill level or budget
- Best courses: Caledonia, True Blue, Dunes Club (member, but know-someone access)
- Nightlife: Broadway at the Beach, beach bars
- Cost estimate: $700-1,200/person for 3-4 days
- Group tip: ideal when half the group is casual golfers

**3. Bandon Dunes, Oregon — Best Pure Golf Trip** (~200 words)
- Why: when the whole group is serious golfers who want nothing but exceptional golf
- GTI trip: /trips/bandon-dunes (full details, course rankings, booking guide)
- Note: no nightlife — Bandon is a remote town; the "nightlife" is the lodge fire pit and a cold beer post-round
- Cost estimate: $2,000-3,000/person for 4 days (all-in, on property)
- Group tip: 4-8 people; everyone should be a committed golfer

**4. Las Vegas, Nevada — Best Nightlife-First Group** (~150 words)
- Why: when golf is the excuse but Vegas is the real draw
- Best courses: Shadow Creek (expensive but spectacular), Las Vegas Paiute, Wynn Golf Club
- Cost estimate: $1,000-2,000/person depending on casino hotel vs. budget stay
- Group tip: book golf early; Shadow Creek has limited tee times

**5. Pinehurst, North Carolina — Best Serious Group** (~150 words)
- Why: American golf history + championship conditions + peaceful setting
- GTI trip: /trips/pinehurst
- Best courses: No. 2 (requires resort stay), No. 4, Tobacco Road nearby
- Cost estimate: $1,200-1,800/person for 3-4 days
- Group tip: 4-8 people who appreciate course architecture and golf history

### Planning Tips for Bachelor Party Golf Trips (350 words)
- **Assign a trip captain:** one person books everything and collects money upfront
- **2 rounds max:** don't over-schedule; one solid round + one recovery round per day is the ceiling
- **Book accommodation before courses:** group houses fill faster than tee times
- **Scramble format:** for mixed-skill groups, scramble keeps pace of play up and keeps frustrated golfers engaged
- **Don't book the hardest course Day 1:** ease the group in, save the marquee round for Day 2 when everyone's loose
- **Budget for incidentals:** range balls, range cart rentals, halfway house food, tips add 15-20% to golf cost
- **Collect money before the trip:** Venmo requests post-trip create friction; collect deposits when booking
