## Outline

### Intro (150 words)
- Hook: the biggest planning mistake is booking flights before checking tee time availability
- State the 6 steps covered
- Link to /trips (for destination ideas)

### Step 1: Choose Your Destination (300 words)
- Match destination to the group's skill level and interests
- Use GTI rankings as the starting framework: /trips
- Key factors: course quality vs. cost tier, walking vs. cart, resort vs. DIY stay
- Point to the 5 state guides as starting points (internal links)
- Mention that off-property stays at places like Streamsong or Bandon Dunes vs. on-property changes the experience significantly

### Step 2: Set a Realistic Budget (300 words)
- Break down the 5 cost buckets: green fees, lodging, travel, food, incidentals
- Budget tiers: $500-800/person (budget), $1,000-1,500 (mid), $2,000-3,500 (premium), $4,000+ (bucket list)
- Link to /articles/budget-golf-trips-usa for the budget-focused version
- Mention that GTI's cost tier rating (1-5) on each trip page reflects the realistic total

### Step 3: Book in the Right Order (250 words)
- Counter-intuitive: book tee times BEFORE flights
- Booking windows for GTI-ranked trips:
  - Bandon Dunes: 12 months out for peak (May-Sept)
  - Pinehurst: resort stay required; book 6-9 months out
  - Sand Valley: 6 months out for summer weekends
  - Streamsong: 3-4 months for peak season
- Use the individual trip pages (/trips/[slug]) for exact tee time rules

### Step 4: Plan the Itinerary (250 words)
- Rule: no more than 2 rounds per day on a serious golf trip
- Build in a rest day on trips 4+ days long
- Morning vs. afternoon tee times: pros and cons by destination (morning for firm conditions at links; afternoon for desert after heat breaks)
- Group size: 4 is ideal for pace and logistics; 8+ needs a trip captain

### Step 5: Book Lodging (200 words)
- On-property vs. off: on-property wins for experience (Bandon, Streamsong, Pinehurst); off-property wins for cost at destinations with many nearby courses (Myrtle Beach, Scottsdale)
- Vacation rentals for groups of 6+
- Mention that GTI's lodging section on each trip page covers the specific options

### Step 6: Pack and Prepare (200 words)
- Link to /articles/golf-trip-packing-list for the full list
- Key items: travel bag/hard case, rain gear for coastal destinations, sun protection for desert
- Logistics: shipping clubs vs. checking (cost comparison)
- Arrive a day early for multi-round itineraries

### Quick Reference Checklist (formatted as table or checklist)
- 12+ months out: check tee time availability for peak destinations
- 9 months: book tee times at high-demand courses
- 6 months: book lodging (especially on-property resort stays)
- 3 months: book flights
- 1 month: confirm all reservations, check cancellation policies
- 2 weeks: finalize packing, arrange club shipping if needed

### Closing (100 words)
- Reinforce the "book backward" principle
- CTA: browse GTI's ranked trips to find your destination → /trips
