### The Best Value Golf Destinations Ranked (main section, ~900 words)

**1. RTJ Golf Trail, Alabama** (~200 words)
- Why it's exceptional value: Pete Dye-level design at $50-80/round
- Courses: Capitol Hill (The Judge), Ross Bridge, Oxmoor Valley
- Cost estimate: ~$600-800/person for 3 days (lodging + golf, no flights)
- Best for: serious golfers who prioritize course quality
- Limitation: no bucket-list name recognition; you're playing great golf no one has heard of

**2. Sand Valley, Wisconsin (shoulder season)** (~200 words)
- Why shoulder: October and early May green fees drop ~30-40%
- Courses: Sand Valley, Mammoth Dunes, Sedge Valley, Lido
- Cost estimate: ~$900-1,200/person in October (vs. $1,600+ peak summer)
- GTI trip link: /trips/sand-valley
- Best for: architecture fans who want multiple rounds at one destination

**3. Myrtle Beach, South Carolina** (~200 words)
- Why it's the volume-value play: 90+ courses, competition keeps prices low
- Best value courses: Caledonia, True Blue, TPC Myrtle Beach
- Cost estimate: ~$600-900/person for 3-4 days
- Limitation: not a bucket-list destination; it's a quantity-over-quality play
- Best for: groups where skill levels vary, first-time golf trippers

**4. Michigan: Arcadia Bluffs in September** (~150 words)
- September pricing is 20-30% below peak; conditions still excellent
- GTI trip link: /trips/arcadia-bluffs
- Cost estimate: ~$900-1,100/person for 3 days

**5. Pinehurst, North Carolina (off-peak winter)** (~150 words)
- November-February: rates drop significantly, course conditions are solid in the South
- GTI trip link: /trips/pinehurst
- Cost estimate: ~$700-1,000/person for 3 days (vs. $1,500+ spring peak)

### How to Cut Costs Without Cutting Quality (350 words)
- **Travel timing:** shoulder season (Oct-Nov, late March for Southeast) cuts rates 20-40%
- **Stay off-property:** saves $100-200/night at resort destinations; use for Scottsdale, Myrtle Beach
- **Replay rates:** many courses offer discounted second-round same day ($40-60 vs. full price)
- **Tuesday-Thursday:** weekday tee times are 15-30% cheaper at most public courses
- **Package deals:** resort packages bundle lodging + rounds at 10-20% discount vs. booking separately
- **Avoid peak months:** Bandon Dunes peak (June-Sept) vs. off-peak (Oct-May) saves $100+/round

### Budget vs. Premium: What You Actually Give Up (200 words)
- Honest framing: budget golf is real quality golf at accessible prices
- What you give up: on-property lodge experience, caddie programs, marquee names
- What you keep: excellent course design, great conditions at value destinations, the core golf experience
